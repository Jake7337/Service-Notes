Service Plan 1: IHCS - Independence and Life Skills

Outcome Period: April 21, 2025 to April 20, 2026

Outcome Statement: John has the confidence and skills needed to be as independent as possible by figuring out what he can do for himself and learning what he needs help with, so that he can take care of himself and feel more in control of his life.

Outcome Actions — Support staff will:

  • Provide clear and simple instructions
  • Offer choices in daily tasks
  • Use visual aids like schedules and checklists
  • Incorporate new interests by exploring activities with John
  • Introduce easy-to-use budgeting tools
  • Encourage problem-solving through trial and error
  • Build confidence with positive reinforcement by celebrating small successes
  • Provide emotional support by offering reassurance and addressing any anxiety or fear John may have
  • Offer reminders for John to take care of his hygiene needs

Service Plan 2: Companion - Routine and Quality of Life

Outcome Period: April 21, 2025 to April 20, 2026

Outcome Statement: John keeps a consistent routine, making time for work and play, so that he keeps living the life he enjoys most.

Outcome Actions — Support staff will:

  • Offer specific choice/s instead of asking vague questions like "What do you want to do?" to help reduce anxiety
  • Remind John to write down his schedule and plans with work or friends
  • Provide necessary supervision in community settings to ensure his health and safety
  • Provide supervision to ensure John takes his medication correctly
  • Schedule medical appointments and follow up to ensure attendance and compliance with recommendations
  • Offer reminders for John to take care of his hygiene needs
